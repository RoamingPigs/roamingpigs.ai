Tactical Radio Channel Simulator (TRCS)

Characterizing vocoder parameter distortion, fricative collapse, and autoregressive hallucination loops across operational narrowband channels.

Autoregressive sequence-to-sequence speech foundation models fail catastrophically when deployed over real-world radio channels. Non-speech acoustic transients and squelch tail bursts trigger continuous ungrounded hallucinations (up to 160 WPM), while 3.4 kHz bandpass filtering collapses high-frequency fricatives (/s/ → /f, h, t/), corrupting tactical callsigns. We designed the Tactical Radio Channel Simulator (TRCS) to benchmark, evaluate, and harden speech foundation models under laboratory clean-room conditions.

Verifier Covariance & Optimal Stopping (VEC-SCR)

Neyman-Pearson stopping boundaries under non-zero verifier error covariance (ΣAB > 0) and asymmetric false-acceptance risk.

Modern test-time compute systems commonly deploy dual verifiers to validate generated solutions. However, naive implementations assume verifier errors are conditionally independent (ρ = 0). When verifiers share pretraining corpora or architectural inductive biases, error covariance is strictly positive (ΣAB > 0). Under asymmetric loss, where accepting an incorrect solution (False Acceptance, CFA) is 10× to 100× more destructive than rejecting a valid one (False Rejection, CFR), assuming independence leads to severe premature stopping and catastrophic verification leakage.

Unified Memory APU Inference & Joules/Token Scaling

Evaluating memory bus bandwidth saturation (256-bit LPDDR5X), wall power dissipation, and physical thermodynamic limits of sovereign inference.

Datacenter GPU clusters carry immense power overhead: host CPU idle draw (250W–400W), PCIe interconnect hops, redundant system memory, and facility cooling overhead (1.15×–1.4× PUE).
