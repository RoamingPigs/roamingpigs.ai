Law of Total Error Covariance and Monotonic Stopping Boundaries

Theory & Proofs

Scaling test-time compute (Best-of-N, tree search, self-correction) relies on automated verification. Standard consensus algorithms assume verifier errors are conditionally independent. Under real-world models and coding tasks, verifiers exhibit positive error covariance (ρ > 0.75), which severely distorts posterior log-odds and triggers catastrophic false acceptance cascades. We formulated the Law of Total Error Covariance and proved that calibrated abstention eliminates false acceptance loops.
