The Progressive 4-Stage Generation Pipeline

Running heavy models for rough drafts wastes energy. We enforce a strictly gated 4-stage pipeline running in memory (/dev/shm/imagine) on the Warden APU.

Stage 1 · Rapid Rough Layout

Z-Image-Turbo 6B (8 steps, ~14s, 512×896)

Ultra-fast initial exploration validating framing, composition, color palettes, and blocking before committing heavy compute.

Stage 2 · Identity & Feature Anchoring

FLUX.2 klein 9B (4 steps, cfg 1.0, Euler, ~32s)

Bakes canonical reference features, facial geometry, and high-contrast structural fidelity into the candidate scene.

Stage 3 · Multi-Modal Conditioning & Editing

Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 20B (20 steps, cfg 2.5, flow-shift 3, easycache 0.2, ~75s)

Instruction-guided precise editing, inpainting, and textual coherence refinement under Flow-Shift sampling.

Stage 4 · Production Upscaling

RealESRGAN x4plus (~4.5s) to 1080×1920 or 4K

High-frequency perceptual sharpening and clean-room upscaling to broadcast resolution without generative drift.

The Four-Tier AI Infrastructure Continuum

How we balance sovereign $0.00 in-house silicon, local Xeon sharding, edge worker microservices, and burst cloud training.

Tier 1 · Sovereign APU (Warden APU)

AMD Strix Halo APU · 122.7 GB Unified LPDDR5X · Vulkan

In-RAM generation (/dev/shm/imagine), local diffusion (flux2, edit, zimage), video (wan2.1, liveportrait), voice synthesis (kokoro), and local reasoning (qwen3-vl:30b at 65 tok/s, qwen3.5:122b). Strictly $0.00 cloud spend.

Tier 2 · Data Sharding & Mastering (Haywire Host)

Dual Xeon Platinum 8160 · 96 Cores · 768 GB RAM

Massive multi-threaded audio sharding, EBU R128 audio mastering, FFmpeg Ken Burns 9:16 video assembly, de-stalling, and YuNet+SFace face embedding verification.

Tier 3 · Edge API Platform (Ambie.ai)

Self-Owned Cloudflare Workers AI Architecture

15 LLMs (Brave search grounded + verified), Whisper Large v3 ASR (word timestamps, direct SRT/VTT), Llama 3.3 70B translation, and Aura 2 TTS.

Tier 4 · Spot GPU Fleet (Vastops)

Automated Spot Provisioning · $0.30–$1.50/hr · $2.00 Ceiling

Automated Vast.ai and RunPod spot provisioning for spokesmodel LoRA training and full-parameter fine-tuning with strict 30-min idle watchdog and PRG eval gates.
